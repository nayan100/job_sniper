# Strategic Outreach Plan: The Zero-Cost "Sniper" Strategy

This plan focuses on a "Quality over Volume" approach, targeting **10 high-match companies per day**. The goal is to minimize costs while maximizing interview conversion by positioning yourself as a **Technical Transformation Partner** through extreme personalization.

---

## Phase 1: Preparation (The "Deterministic Proof")
Hardware VPs are skeptical of "AI Speed." You must demonstrate **Reliability**.

1.  **The "Safety-Traces" Portfolio:**
    *   Create a GitHub repo showing how AI generates **MISRA-C compliant unit tests** or **functional safety (ISO 26262) documentation**.
    *   Use `asciinema` to record CLI traces of architectural audits. This proves your workflow is real and reproducible.
2.  **The "Reliability" Resume:**
    *   Focus on "Deterministic Gains."
    *   *Example:* "Reduced audit time for safety-critical firmware by 40% using agentic validation pipelines."

---

## Phase 2: The Zero-Cost Infrastructure
No burner domains or paid subscriptions. Use "High-Trust" personal infrastructure.

1.  **The "Professional" Gmail:**
    *   Use a dedicated, clean email address (e.g., `nayan.systems.eng@gmail.com` or `nayan.embedded.io@gmail.com`).
    *   Set up a clean, text-based professional signature with links to your GitHub and LinkedIn.
2.  **The Lead Extraction Stack (Free Tiers):**
    *   **Apollo.io / Hunter.io:** Use free tiers (50 credits/mo) to find exact VP/CTO emails.
    *   **Clearbit Connect:** Chrome extension for finding emails directly in Gmail.
    *   **Gemini 1.5 Flash:** Use the free tier to analyze company 10-Ks, engineering blogs, and job descriptions for "Pain Points."

---

## Phase 3: The "Sniper" Workflow (10 Companies/Day)

| Time | Action | Tool |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **09:00 - 10:00** | **Identify & Extract:** Pick 10 companies. Find the VP of Engineering/CTO and the Talent Lead. | LinkedIn / Apollo |
| **10:00 - 11:30** | **Deep Research:** Use Gemini to find a specific technical challenge they face (e.g., "Scaling MQTT for 100k devices" or "Transitioning to Rust for Safety"). | Gemini / Google |
| **11:30 - 13:00** | **The Technical Hook:** Draft 10 highly personalized emails. Mention a specific product or public engineering hurdle. | Gmail |
| **14:00 - 15:00** | **LinkedIn Warm-up:** Follow the 10 VPs. Comment thoughtfully on one of their posts or a company update. | LinkedIn |

## Phase 5: Interview Prep & Presentation
Because you only apply to 10/day, use the saved time to prepare for the "Hit."

1.  **The "First 90 Days" Slide:** For every company that responds, create a 3-slide deck on how you will automate their specific compliance/testing bottleneck.
2.  **The Live Audit:** Be ready to perform a live "Technical Audit" using your AI-native tools during the interview.

---

## Phase 6: KPI (The Sniper Metrics)
*   **Daily Goal:** 10 "Best Match" VPs reached.
*   **Weekly Goal:** 50 VPs + 50 HR Follow-ups.
*   **Success Metric:** 10% Response Rate (5 high-quality interviews per week) due to extreme personalization and the "Technical Hook."
