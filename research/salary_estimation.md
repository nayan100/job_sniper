# Salary Estimation: AI-Native Embedded & IoT Roles (2026)

Roles that specifically require **AI CLI mastery** command a significant premium (15–20%) over traditional engineering roles due to the drastic increase in productivity (30–60% faster delivery).

## 1. Embedded DevOps Engineer (AI-Augmented)

| Experience Level | Base Salary (US) | Total Compensation (TC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Junior (0-2 yrs)** | $105,000 – $130,000 | $115k – $145k |
| **Mid (3-6 yrs)** | $140,000 – $175,000 | $160k – $200k |
| **Senior (7+ yrs)** | $180,000 – $220,000 | $230k – $320k+ |

## 2. Industrial IoT Solutions Architect (AI-Native)

| Experience Level | Base Salary (US) | Total Compensation (TC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mid-Senior** | $155,000 – $195,000 | $180k – $230k |
| **Principal / Lead** | $220,000 – $300,000 | $280k – $450k+ |

## 3. Global Variations (Senior Roles)
*   **Europe (Berlin/London):** €90,000 – €140,000
*   **India (MNC/Startup):** ₹25 – ₹55+ Lakhs (Highly variable for AI-native talent)
*   **Remote (Global):** $120,000 – $180,000

## 4. The "AI Multiplier"
Candidates who demonstrate **"Prompting Mastery"** and **"Agentic Workflow Design"** are often hired at a higher level (e.g., Senior hired as Principal) or given "AI Performance Bonuses" based on the delivery speed gains they achieve.
